Duration and intake

  • International students can study full-time.
  • Standard duration: 2 years full-time.
  • Advanced standing can reduce the full-time duration to 1.5 years for eligible prior study.
  • Published next intakes: February and July.

2027 international fee

  • Indicative international tuition: AUD 47,040 per year.
  • RMIT describes the figure as an indicative annual fee based on a standard full-time study load.
  • Fees are adjusted annually, so the dataset records the fee year explicitly.

Entry requirements

  • Relevant Australian bachelor's degree or international equivalent in computer science, data science, artificial intelligence, software engineering, information technology or statistics with GPA at least 2.0/4.0.
  • An alternative consideration pathway may apply to a bachelor's degree plus at least three years of relevant professional experience.
  • English requirement includes IELTS Academic 6.5 overall with no band below 6.0, or an approved equivalent.
