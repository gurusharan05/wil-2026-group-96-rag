Accommodation support

  • RMIT provides information and support for students arriving from overseas or interstate.
  • Housing options include purpose-built student accommodation and other rental options in Melbourne.
  • Student Welfare Advisors can provide information about housing options, rental and tenancy issues, moving in/out, and financial support/budgeting.

Purpose-built student accommodation

  • RMIT lists multiple purpose-built student accommodation providers around its Melbourne campuses.
  • Examples on the current RMIT accommodation page include Iglu, Scape and UniLodge properties.

RAG use

Use this source for questions about RMIT-specific accommodation support and housing options. Do not treat provider availability or prices as permanent; those details should be refreshed.
