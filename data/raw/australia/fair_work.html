Workplace rights

  • International students have basic workplace rights and protections under Australian workplace law.
  • Migration status does not remove workplace entitlements.
  • Minimum pay and conditions can depend on the applicable award, agreement or contract.

Pay and conditions

  • Employees receive minimum entitlements under the National Employment Standards where applicable.
  • Award-covered workers may have minimum pay rates, penalty rates and allowances specific to the relevant award.
