Student visa work condition

  • Student visa holders cannot work before starting their course.
  • While the course is in session, student visa holders can generally work up to 48 hours per fortnight.
  • Different rules apply to certain research master's and doctoral students; the exact visa conditions should always be checked for the individual visa holder.
