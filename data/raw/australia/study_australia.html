Working while studying

  • International students can work while studying in Australia subject to applicable restrictions.
  • Study Australia describes a 48-hours-per-fortnight limit during study terms/semesters.
  • International students have workplace protections like other workers.

Accommodation

  • Study Australia describes short-term accommodation, renting a house or apartment, and shared housing as common options for international students.
  • Students should consider support from their education provider when finding accommodation.
