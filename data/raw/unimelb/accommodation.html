University accommodation

  • The University offers accommodation options near the Parkville campus.
  • Options include university apartment-style accommodation and residential colleges.
  • Accommodation can include furnished rooms, utilities, internet and support services depending on residence.

2027 accommodation examples

  • The published 2027 page lists Lisa Bellear House, Little Hall, The Lofts, International House and Wilam Hall with different weekly cost ranges and inclusions.
  • The University also lists partner accommodation and other housing options such as private rentals and sharehouses.
