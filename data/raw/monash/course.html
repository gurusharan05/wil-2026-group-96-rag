Course identity

  • Course: Master of Data Science
  • Course code: C6004
  • CRICOS: 085349A
  • Campus: Clayton

Duration and intake

  • Entry level 1: 2 years full-time / 4 years part-time.
  • Entry level 2: 1.5 years full-time / 3 years part-time.
  • Published application periods include Semester 1 (February) and Semester 2 (July).

2027 international fee

  • 2027 international fee: AUD 57,540 per 48 credit points.
  • Monash describes 48 credit points as a standard full-time course load for one year.
  • Fees are subject to annual change.

Entry requirements

  • Entry level 1: Australian bachelor's degree or equivalent, not necessarily in IT, with at least a 60% average.
  • Entry level 2: a cognate IT-related degree or specified IT/math background, with at least a 60% average.
  • English requirement includes IELTS Academic 6.5 overall with minimum 6.0 in each band, or equivalent.
